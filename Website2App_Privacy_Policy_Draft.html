1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Developer / Company Legal Name] (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use Website2App Studio (“the App”), including its website-to-app conversion, project management, cloud build and publishing-assistance features.

By using the App, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, authentication identifiers and profile settings, depending on the sign-in methods you choose.

Website and project information: website URLs you submit, project names, app names, package identifiers, customization settings, assets you upload, and conversion or build configuration.

Website content processed for conversion: pages, images, scripts, metadata and other publicly accessible or otherwise authorized content that the conversion service needs to inspect. Do not submit websites or content unless you own them or have permission to use them.

Build and diagnostic information: build status, timestamps, error reports, device/app version information, crash logs and technical logs needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Payment information: subscription status, transaction references and billing details supplied by the payment provider. We do not intend to store full payment-card credentials; payment processing is handled by the applicable payment provider.

Support communications: information you provide when contacting us.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage your account; analyse submitted websites; generate, test, store and deliver APK/AAB build files; maintain project history; provide customer support; process subscriptions and payments; protect the App against fraud, abuse and security threats; improve reliability; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the App, such as cloud hosting and storage providers, authentication and database providers, AI or website-analysis providers, build infrastructure providers, crash/analytics services and payment processors. They may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and subject to appropriate contractual or legal protections.

If you choose to connect a Google Play Console account, information necessary for the publishing action may be exchanged with Google through the permissions you authorize. We do not publish an app or submit a release without your explicit instruction.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Website Access and User Responsibilities

The App may retrieve website resources you ask it to analyse. It is not intended to bypass passwords, CAPTCHAs, paywalls, robots restrictions or other access controls. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions needed to submit, process, convert and distribute a website and its content.

Do not submit passwords, private keys, payment credentials or other secrets in project fields unless a feature specifically requests them through a secure mechanism. Any optional website credentials must be handled securely and only used for the conversion task you authorize.

6. Files, APKs, AABs and Signing Keys

Generated project files and build artifacts may be stored in cloud infrastructure so that you can download them or access your build history. They may be retained for the period described in Section 8.

If the App supports keystore or signing-key storage, those credentials must be protected using appropriate encryption and access controls. Never share your keystore password.
